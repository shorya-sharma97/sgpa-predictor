# 🎯 End-Sem Grade Target & CGPA Calculator

A simple, dark-mode, mobile-friendly tool to figure out exactly how many marks you need in your **75-mark end-sem exam** to hit your target grade (and see how it affects your SGPA/CGPA in real time).

> ⚡ **Built with pure vibecoding & minimum effort** — just wanted a clean, fast tool that does the math without opening a spreadsheet every exam season.

## ✨ Features
* 📊 **Live Target Calculation** — Enter internals (`/60`), set credits, and slide to your target grade (`C` to `O`) to see the exact raw marks needed out of `75`.
* 🧮 **Real-Time SGPA & CGPA** — Updates instantly at the top as you tweak grades.
* 🚫 **Impossible Grade Detection** — Tells you right away if a grade is mathematically out of reach and lets you cap it in one click.
* 💾 **Auto-Save** — Uses `localStorage` so your subjects and marks stay saved across reloads and restarts.
* 🌑 **Flat Dark UI** — Clean, distraction-free, and built for mobile screens.

## 🚀 Tech Stack
* Single-file **HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JS** — zero frameworks, zero build steps, zero bloat.

## 🛠️ Usage
Just open the live link, add your subjects, and start sliding.
